# TriGo — Business Decisions Log

Business decisions made during development that are not yet reflected in
`docs/SRS.md` and `docs/SPMP.md`. Each entry must be carried into those documents
before the next revision is submitted. Mark an entry **Done** once both are updated.

| Date | Decision | Affects | SRS/SPMP updated |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-05 | **Booking requires a road route.** A fare is only calculated from the OpenRouteService road distance; if no road route is available the passenger cannot book (no straight-line fare bookings). | Booking, fare | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **Out-of-area: one driver per request.** A request is offered to one driver at a time. ~~The driver proposes a fare;~~ the passenger accepts or declines (fare part superseded 2026-10-11, see below). There is no counter-offer. | Out-of-area trips | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **Out-of-area covers pickup OR destination outside Trinidad.** A trip is out-of-area if either end is outside the Trinidad, Bohol service area. | Out-of-area trips, service area | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **Out-of-area requests expire after 30 minutes.** | Out-of-area trips | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **Standard fare is fixed.** The passenger pays the fare shown at confirmation. It is calculated from the admin-managed per-km fare settings (base fare + per-km rate per vehicle type). It is labelled "Fare", not "Estimated fare". | Booking, fare, admin tariff management | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **Barangay tariffs from Poblacion** (approved; supersedes the earlier PENDING entry). A trip between Poblacion and a barangay, in either direction, is charged that barangay's fixed tariff, set per vehicle type and charged per trip. Every other trip uses the per-km fare. | Fare, admin tariff management | No |
| 2026-10-11 | **Out-of-area fare is the admin per-km fare and is FINAL.** Drivers no longer propose an amount: a driver accepts the request at the per-km fare and the passenger confirms that driver or chooses another. Barangay tariffs never apply to out-of-area trips. Labelled "Fare" everywhere (no "agreed" or "reference" fare). | Out-of-area trips, fare | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **Fares keep centavos.** Fares are shown and stored to the centavo (e.g. ₱40.92); they are not rounded to whole pesos. | Fare, payments | No |
| 2026-10-05 | ~~**Passenger cannot contact the driver yet.**~~ (superseded 2026-10-13, see below) The booking status screen shows the driver's name, vehicle, plate and rating only; no phone number or call/message button. Contacting the driver is a planned separate feature. | Booking status, driver privacy | No |
| 2026-10-13 | **Contact numbers are shared only between the passenger and the assigned driver while the trip is active.** During an accepted, arrived or in-progress trip the passenger sees the driver's number and the driver sees the passenger's name and number, each with a Call button. Nobody else (e.g. drivers browsing open requests) can read them, and sharing stops when the trip is completed or cancelled. | Booking status, driver trip, privacy | No |
| 2026-10-13 | **Driver Home shows fares collected, not earnings.** "Fares collected" (Today / This week, since Monday 00:00) is the sum of the driver's own completed trips' `finalFare`, labelled "Before commission". It is not net earnings. Commission is not shown until the commission feature exists. | Driver Home, earnings, commission | No |
| 2026-10-13 | **Commission policy (final).** Admin-set PERCENTAGE of the final fare on every completed trip (standard and out-of-area). Admin sets the payment period (e.g. 3 or 7 days); the period total is due at its end. Driver pays via GCash to TriGo's admin number or cash at the office and enters the reference number; admin confirms on the admin website. Unpaid after the due date: warning shown to the driver; after the admin-set grace period: suspended from accepting bookings (can still log in and see the balance) until the admin marks it paid; suspension lifts automatically. Permanent ban only by the admin manually. Supersedes the earlier 24-hour settlement idea. | Commission, driver payments, admin, driver suspension | No |
| 2026-10-05 | **PENDING — iOS photo/camera permission text.** The iOS camera and photo-library descriptions in `app.config.ts` mention only driver-application documents. Update them to also cover the passenger profile photo in the next EAS build (native change; Android does not use this text). | Profile photo, iOS build | No |
| 2026-10-14 | **Barangay tariffs are separate documents.** Stored as `barangayTariffs/{barangayId}` {name, tricycle, motorcycle, active, updatedAt, updatedBy}, admin-only writes, readable by signed-in users (one write per barangay; a single map exceeded the rules expression limit). Unsaved barangays have no tariff and use per-km. Barangay IDs/names are duplicated in `firestore.rules` and `admin/src/tariffs/barangays.ts` (and later the mobile app) - change all together. | Fare, admin tariff management | No |
- 2026-10-14: Admins (custom claim admin: true) can read all user profiles, bookings and driver records for management and reports; admins cannot edit bookings. Admin access is limited to accounts granted by the setAdminClaim script.
- 2026-10-14: Admins can read all out-of-area requests (Out-of-Area Trips page), read-only; admins cannot edit or cancel them. Open requests past expiresAt are shown as Expired on the admin website (the app never writes status 'expired').
- 2026-10-14: Admins can read every driver record (including revoked/unverified) and revoke or restore verification on the Drivers page. Revoking requires a reason (admin-only, stored as drivers.verificationNote with verificationUpdatedAt/By) and sets isVerified, isOnline and isAvailable to false in one write; a driver revoked mid-trip can still finish that trip. Restoring clears the reason and leaves the driver offline. The mobile app shows a revoked driver "Driver access revoked. Please contact the TriGo administrator." (never the reason). A passenger can no longer confirm an out-of-area driver who was revoked after accepting; the app returns the request to searching.
- 2026-10-14: Reports use Firestore count/sum aggregates only (no booking documents downloaded). A completed or cancelled booking counts on the day of its updatedAt (completion/cancellation time; such bookings cannot be updated afterwards), like the driver app's fares summary. Ranges up to 62 days are charted per day, longer ones (max 366 days) per calendar month. Commission on the report is an estimate at the current rate. Charts are plain SVG (no chart library); CSV export is per-period rows and a summary.
- 2026-10-14: System settings (admin website). Admins edit fareSettings/active.outOfArea as one complete object: accept out-of-area requests on/off, request expiry 1-30 whole minutes, driver search radius 1-50 whole km (a save changes either fares or outOfArea, never both). The app picks them up the next time it loads fare settings; requests already sent keep their expiry. Support contacts are separate documents supportContacts/{id} {label, phone, active, sortOrder, updatedAt, updatedBy}; admins add, edit, hide and delete them; signed-in app users read only active ones, shown on the passenger Help & Support screen (lowest order first) with tap-to-call.
PENDING: Firebase Storage needs the Blaze plan. Blocks profile photos AND driver application document uploads. Code parked on branch feature/profile-photo.
- 2026-10-14: Barangay tariffs are stored as separate docs barangayTariffs/{barangayId} {name, tricycle, motorcycle, active, updatedAt, updatedBy}, admin-only writes, readable by signed-in users (one write per barangay; a single map exceeded the rules expression limit). Unsaved barangays have no tariff and use per-km. Barangay IDs/names are duplicated in firestore.rules and admin/src/tariffs/barangays.ts (and later the mobile app) - change all together.
- 2026-10-19: Commission payments and earnings: real money is not moved by TriGo. Drivers pay commission by GCash only (outside the app) to TriGo's GCash account and submit the amount and GCash reference number; the admin verifies manually and confirms or rejects. Admin cash-outs (GCash, Maya, bank transfer) happen outside the app and are only recorded in TriGo. TriGo's GCash payment account (name and number) is an admin setting in System Settings, shown to drivers. Automatic payment/disbursement through a payment provider (e.g. PayMongo) is a future enhancement requiring a registered business and Blaze.
- 2026-10-28: Barangay boundaries: data/trinidad-barangays.geojson, 20 barangays of Trinidad, Bohol, extracted from the Philippines Subnational Administrative Boundaries (adm4, version v03, valid from 2025-02-13), PSA/NAMRIA via OCHA HDX. Credit the source in documentation and the app's About screen; check the license on the HDX dataset page.- 2026-10-28: Barangay boundaries are mapped to the tariff IDs by name in src/constants/barangayBoundaries.ts (generated by scripts/buildBarangayBoundaries.mjs; [latitude, longitude], 5 decimals), 19 barangays plus 'poblacion' (PSA writes it plainly as "Poblacion", PSGC 0701244015). PSA code 0701244011 does not exist in the source. findBarangayAt() picks the barangay containing a point, or the nearest one within the 150 m service-area edge tolerance; not used for fares yet. The mobile barangay list (src/constants/barangays.ts), the admin list and firestore.rules barangayNames() are checked identical by npm run test:barangays.
